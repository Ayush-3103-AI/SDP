# ADR-0008 — Own recorded induced-defect data replaces the REU inheritance

STATUS:      superseded by ADR-0009
DATE:        2026-10-27
REVERSIBLE:  costly (it decides what data every evaluation rests on)
SUPERSEDES:  charter §4 "inherited 6,454-image dataset" and "YOLO11-S best.pt" (00-charter Amendment 1);
             assumptions A1, A2, A7 (01-understanding)

## Context (evidence, 2026-10-26/27)
- The user confirmed that the Drive folder "Camera Roll" is the entire REU hand-over. It holds 5,490 1280×720
  webcam stills plus 9 mp4 clips, all from 2025-04-29, 11:20–17:52: one print of one white PLA part.
  There are no labels, splits, induction log or weights (docs/inherited/camera_roll_report.html).
- The printer in the photos has a dual-hotend toolhead in an enclosed industrial frame. That matches the
  Aion 500 + webcam + YOLOv8 + Roboflow setup in AM_VM_SME_2619_Paper_17.04.2026.md, not the deck's
  Ender 5 Plus + Pi HQ + YOLO11-S. The paper's 4,500 labelled Roboflow frames were never handed over.
- The repo's inherited models:
  - AM_1_Model_1.pt: YOLOv8n **detector**, 1 class `defect`, trained 2025-05-06
  - AM_2_Model_2.pt: YOLOv8 **image classifier**; classes OK, blobs, cracks, spaghetti, stringing and
    under-extrusion (×2 spellings); trained 2025-11-05
- Consequences:
  - K1 (charter §3) fires: 0 % of defect boxes can be joined to an induced cause, because there are none.
  - A2 (reproduce mAP50 0.828) is impossible: no test split, no YOLO11-S.
  - A7 (REU split leakage) is moot.
- Already built and unaffected: the loop, gate, explainer plumbing, UI and study code
  (24 tickets, 208 tests). They never depended on which dataset arrives.

## Options
1. **Charter K1 pivot as written.** Drop the learned model and use the prior-based explanation (ADR-0002
   option 1). No new data. But that fallback's accuracy is defined "on the same LOGO folds", which don't
   exist. S1/S2 become unmeasurable, there is no detector, and the project can't show anything beyond a demo.
2. **Simulation-only project.** FakePrinter + replay of the camera roll. Measurable for S3/S4 only. Drops the
   "root cause" half of 4.1.3.
3. **Generate our own ground truth (recommended).** A recorded, induced-defect data campaign on our printer.
   The induction log is written by construction at the moment of induction, so the image↔trial join is
   exact. We label sampled frames for the detector, train YOLO11-S ourselves and re-run K1/K2 on our data.
   ADR-0002 option 1 (the prior-based score) is kept as the mandatory floor on the same folds.

## Decision (proposed) — option 3, with these calls
- **D1 Data campaign (new, moved to the front).** Open-loop: no correction runs, the loop only records.
  - Matrix: 4 geometries × 8 conditions (7 actionable causes + `none`) × 2 repeats = **64 short trials**,
    each ~20–30 min with the induction at a fixed layer. That is ≈ 25–30 printer-hours: about 2 weeks
    part-time on one printer, or 1 week on two.
  - `misalignment` (layer shift) is not induced, because it needs mechanical disturbance. It stays a
    detection-only critical class.
  - Each trial = one `trial_id` and one cause. Co-occurrence is not induced (A8 holds by design).
- **D2 Who sends induction commands (a safety-rule exception that must be explicit).**
  - The loop never induces.
  - A human-invoked `xqi induce --trial T --cause C --to X` sends exactly one setpoint G-code via OctoPrint
    and writes the trials.csv row atomically.
  - It is allowed only during a data-campaign trial, with an operator present (CLAUDE.md hard rule: real
    printer, user present), and it is outside ADR-0004's gate by design: inductions deliberately leave the
    L3 window, though never L1.
  - L1 physical limits still apply; induce refuses beyond them.
- **D3 Recording.** `xqi record` = the loop in baseline-off/observe mode. It saves frames (1 fps, fixed
  camera pose) and telemetry with timestamps. trials.csv and images.csv are written directly in the 09 §Data
  format, so T-0005 shrinks from "adapt an unknown REU format" to "export our own logs".
- **D4 Labelling (new HUMAN work).**
  - Sample ~25 frames per trial after the induction, ≈ 1,600 frames.
  - Box-label the 7 defect classes in CVAT or Label Studio and export YOLO format. ≈ 25–30 person-hours,
    split across the team.
  - AM_1 (the 1-class `defect` detector) pre-proposes boxes so humans only fix boxes and assign classes.
  - The camera roll is never in training or eval splits: different printer, no causes. It is used only as a
    replay feed and for demos.
- **D5 Detector.**
  - New ticket: train YOLO11-S at 1088 px on our labels (GPU laptop), split by trial under LOGO.
  - This replaces T-0008 "reproduce the REU baseline".
  - Acceptance is report-only with a floor of mAP50 ≥ 0.50 on held-out geometries [assumed]. FR-003's
    baseline-parity wording changes accordingly.
- **D6 Cause explanation.**
  - The learned image model (ADR-0002 option 2) stays primary if K1 passes on our data. It should, because
    the join is exact by construction.
  - The prior + sensor score (option 1) is always computed on the same folds as the floor and fallback.
  - K2 stays the gate.
  - Small-data risk is high (~64 trials, 4 LOGO folds), so S1/S2 are reported with bootstrap 95 % CIs.
- **D7 Scope cuts to fit ~10 weeks** (semester assumed to end mid-January 2027, STATE Q1):
  - The closed-loop correction campaign (old T-0041, S5) shrinks to ~16 runs: 4 geometries × the 4 most
    frequent causes.
  - Kobra 2 Neo is used only if it is available (T-0050 stays COULD).
  - The operator study (T-0045) stays SHOULD.
- **D8 Metrics re-based** (charter Amendment 1):
  - S1 and S2 are measured on our campaign under 4-fold LOGO, with CIs.
  - S3 and S4 are unchanged.
  - S5 runs on the reduced campaign.
  - A new S6 covers our detector (mAP50 floor 0.50, report-only).

## Consequences
- Critical path becomes: rig bring-up (T-0025 envelope, T-0028 OctoPrint smoke) → `record`/`induce` (CODE)
  → data campaign (HUMAN) → labelling (HUMAN) → T-0005 export → T-0006 audit (K1 on own data) → T-0007 freeze
  → detector + cause model (GPU) → K2 → conformal/CF evals → reduced closed-loop campaign → acceptance → report.
- The printer and humans come first now: nothing in the data lane starts until a real printer runs OctoPrint
  with a fixed camera (K3 risk moves from week 6 to now).
