# ADR-0007 — Frozen evaluation: leave-one-geometry-out, split by trial

STATUS:      accepted (frozen once T-0007 writes data/splits/)
DATE:        2026-10-03
REVERSIBLE:  one-way door once any result has been viewed

## Context
Frames from one trial are near-duplicates. The REU split was "multi-label stratified", probably image-level. The deck asks for "a protocol for testing transfer to unseen geometries".

## Decision
- 8 LOGO folds (one per geometry). Inside the training geometries, a 20 % calibration split **by trial**.
- Both printers are pooled in v1; leave-one-printer-out is COULD (T-0050).
- `data/splits/*.json` is committed and is not edited after T-0007.
- Metrics and thresholds: 06-requirements §Frozen eval.
- Verdict owner: Dr. Meti.

## Consequences
Numbers will be lower than image-level splits; that's the honest figure. Detector mAP is still reported on the REU split for comparability, plus LOGO detector mAP if time allows.
