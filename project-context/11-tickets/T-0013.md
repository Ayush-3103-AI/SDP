  Spec:
    - `train_cause.py --all` for both rungs (probe with `--freeze-backbone`, and fine-tune), then `eval_cause.py --model-dir models/cause`.
    - Gate 2 rung rule: the fine-tune is chosen only if its LOGO mean top-1 beats the probe's by more than the probe's
      fold std; otherwise the probe is the runtime model. K2 is judged on the chosen rung.
    - Report (Gate 4):
      - right after the K2 line, the block `Frame / Baseline / Model / Result / Risks` (Frame quotes the
        cause_model.py docstring; Result is mean ± std on LOGO);
      - per-fold and mean ± std top-1/top-3 vs the floor, for both rungs;
      - per-class recall; confusion matrix; gap-to-oracle;
      - top-1 sliced by defect class and by printer;
      - ECE per fold (the probabilities feed conformal sets and proposal order);
      - worst errors: the 20 most confident wrong test predictions as a crop grid with true/predicted labels
        (reports/cause_errors.png); a human looks at it, and the report records what they saw;
      - staleness: what would make the model stale (a new printer, material, camera position or nozzle) and how it
        would show (larger conformal sets, the T-0048 recalibration).
    - Leakage sanity check: if any fold's top-1 > 0.98, flag "suspected leakage" and stop.
    - Then `train_cause.py --final` for the chosen rung → the runtime model.
    - Gate 5: final.pt is a dict: state_dict, causes, defects, rung, input_px and normalisation, the sha256 of logo.json
      and crops.csv, the git SHA, and the LOGO metrics. CauseModel refuses a checkpoint whose causes or defects order
      differs from the config.
    - Save predictions (chosen rung) so later evaluation is CPU-only: `preds_fold{k}.npz` (probs_calib, y_calib, probs_test, y_test,
      test_defect, test_trial, labels) and `preds_final.npz` (the final model's calibration split).
    - Gate 5 close-out, on CPU after the push: copy 8 crops (2 each from the 4 most frequent causes) into
      tests/fixtures/cause_golden/ with expected.json (top-1 and probabilities from final.pt). test_cause_artifact.py
      loads final.pt through CauseModel, predicts them, and asserts the same top-1 and probabilities within 1e-3.
      It skips if final.pt is absent.
    - 🖥 GPU batch G3; runbook docs/gpu-runs/T-0013.md.
